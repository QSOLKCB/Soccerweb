THE TOUCHLINE MANUAL

Easy to play.
Room to get good.

Tap your kick button to pass. Hold it, then release for power. Aim with your running direction before releasing. Near an opponent with the ball? Press kick to slide tackle, then release before kicking again. A dedicated pass or chip consumes a held kick until release. Use the dedicated pass button for a first-time pass.

Bend it. Hold a curve key at release, then steer with left/right or curve keys during flight. Use chip for a lofted kick. Meet an airborne ball for a volley; move away from the opposition goal as you kick for an overhead attempt.

Play the space. Teammates make runs. Switch to the nearest player, defend the shooting lane, and let your goalkeeper work. Rain changes traction and ball speed. Indoor walls keep the action alive.

Gamepad: left stick / D-pad moves, A kicks, X passes, B chips, Y switches, shoulders bend, Start pauses. Connect two controllers for two players. Keyboard and gamepads may be mixed.

Match rules: two halves, automatic throw-ins, corners and goal kicks. No offside, fouls or cards. Cup and Challenge draws continue with golden goal. The clock stops for set pieces and goal replays.
