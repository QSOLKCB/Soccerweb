## Reproducible gameplay measurements

`node tests/gameplay_report.mjs` runs 12 complete AI matches: both grounds × Club/Pro/Elite × seeds 1988/42, each with 120 active seconds and default teams/clear weather. No scores or positions are injected. [Before JSON](evidence/phase2-before/gameplay-report.json) and [after JSON](evidence/phase2-after/gameplay-report.json) include every score, tick count and final hash.

| Metric, six matches per ground | Outdoor before → after | Indoor before → after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total shots | 24 → 44 | 171 → 171 |
| Total pass attempts | 728 → 393 | 606 → 209 |
| Mean crowded teammate pairs/sample | 0.099 → 0.069 | 0.140 → 0.020 |
| Mean short-possession percentage | 57.2% → 49.8% | 73.0% → 75.2% |
| New completed-pass counter | 315 / 393 | 161 / 209 |

A sample occurs every 30 active simulation ticks; a crowded pair means same-team players within 30 pixels. Short possession means fewer than 20 consecutive owner ticks, averaged per match. Before did not count completed passes, so no completion-rate improvement is claimed. Fewer pass attempts alone is not success: combined with lanes/support, the outdoor sweep produces more shots and fewer brief possessions. The formerly shotless Elite seed-42 outdoor match now takes ten shots and remains a legitimate 0–0. Goals are never prescribed.

Indoor crowding falls, but quick turnover persists and scorelines include 7–1 and 8–2. Those are balance tradeoffs for human testing, not evidence that every metric improved. This small fixed-seed AI sweep does not measure player enjoyment, difficulty fairness or exploit resistance. Seeded scenario tests additionally cover clear goals, physical pass delivery, obstructed lanes, nearest contact, braking/wet traction, support runs, keeper saves, curved shots and damped wall rebounds.

## Audio verification artifacts

`node tests/audio_artifacts.mjs` writes `work/browser/audio/`, uploaded in `browser-evidence`. It includes all five 32-bar JSON event traces, deterministic SFX recipes, manifest hashes and eight-bar WAVs generated by the integer reference DSP. **These WAVs are the reference instrument, not recordings of the live multi-voice Web Audio mix.** Open the running game to evaluate the full soundtrack. Human listening was not performed here.

The original score has intro/verse/lift/turnaround structure, rhythm, bass, lead and harmony; indoor adds arpeggiation. Seed 1988 trace hashes: menu `8a3060ba`, outdoor `ea10d607`, indoor `0016a47e`, goal `fab6f8a6`, victory `3ee0c6e0`. The unchanged integer DSP now receives new score events, so the two-second/32,000-sample reference pin is `7787ba01`. [Audio manifest](evidence/phase2-after/audio-manifest.json) records longer WAV hashes/sample counts. Tests assert deterministic recipes, bounded gain/duration, scheduler catch-up/voice limits, cleanup and serialized audio suspension. Chromium exercises the actual browser graph during gameplay.

## Performance scope

Both comparisons sample 120 requestAnimationFrame intervals per ground in headless Chromium on Ubuntu 24.04 CI, viewport 1365×1000, DPR 1. Baseline mean: 16.666 ms for both grounds; p95: outdoor 16.8 ms, indoor 16.7 ms. After mean: outdoor 16.667 ms, indoor 16.665 ms; p95 16.7 ms for both, maximum 16.8 ms. Full values are in [after timings](evidence/phase2-after/frame-timings.json). A second smoke context checks 900×900 DPR 2/reduced motion and low-resolution backing; it is not a sustained frame benchmark.

Static stadium geometry is cached, sort/trail arrays are reused and DPR is capped at 2. The existing simulation snapshots and some draw-time objects remain allocated. RAF intervals measure delivered cadence in this environment, not GPU cost or ordinary-laptop performance. No human playtest, listening test, physical gamepad test or cross-browser/performance guarantee is claimed.
