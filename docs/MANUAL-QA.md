# Owner acceptance session

**Manual QA: NOT RUN in this implementation environment.** CI keyboard/virtual-gamepad routes and decoded audio samples do not establish enjoyable balance, physical controller compatibility or listening quality. Record browser/version, operating system, hardware, display scale, controller model and findings when running this script. Use the local HTTP server or the verified Pages build after merge.

## Five minutes outdoors

1. Open Practice / Skills. Dribble into the ring, then complete Three passes. Try a very quick tap, a held kick, and a dedicated pass while holding kick. Releasing after that pass should not issue another action. Remap one key and check the instruction updates.
2. Choose Bend a goal. Keep facing up, hold kick for about half a second, then hold right curve while releasing. The same straight shot should hit the near post; retry to compare. Try too little/too much aim and note whether misses are understandable.
3. Choose Read the goalkeeper. Shoot centrally, then aim away from the keeper. A caught ball should stay in the keeper's hands until distribution. Try Win the ball: press kick near the carrier, release, then press again to play the loose ball.
4. Play a one-minute outdoor match at Club, then Pro or Elite. Actively move, pass, aim, curve, defend and switch throughout. Check whether interceptions/selection feel fair; note seed, teams, score and difficulty. Compare rain braking/turning. Export a replay and verify it after returning home.

## Five minutes indoors and devices

1. Play one-minute indoor matches at two difficulties. Make several passes before shooting. Use a wall pass, contest a loose ball, defend the near post, and pressure a keeper who has caught a shot. Log rapid possession exchanges, repeatable scoring patterns and any five-goal margins. A single score is not evidence of overall balance.
2. Start local two-player. Hold both players' movement/action keys simultaneously. Repeat with two standard controllers if available; mix one keyboard and one gamepad. Check A/X/B/Y, shoulders, D-pad, axes and Start.
3. Pause while charging; navigate pause controls with Tab/Enter/Space. Change window focus, unplug a controller and reconnect. Confirm the game pauses and no held input sticks. Check controller slot reassignment before resuming.
4. With headphones at a comfortable level, listen through menu → outdoor/indoor → goal/replay → results/victory. Adjust all three volume controls; mute/unmute; pause/resume and hide/restore the tab. Listen for clicks, excessive crowd/kick levels, cut-off themes or accumulating voices. CI's live-mix WAVs are useful audit captures but do not replace device listening.
5. Resize, select wide/close/pixel/minimal modes and enable system reduced motion. Check ball/goalmouth/HUD visibility. Reload offline after caching. Repeat a short session in Firefox and Safari on real hardware; record frame stutters, device fan/load and accessibility issues.

Keep failures with reproduction steps and replay files where applicable. Do not approve acceptance solely because automated assertions pass. Touch controls, multiplayer networking and replay seeking remain outside this milestone.
